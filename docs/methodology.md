# Methodology

This register follows a standard 5×5 likelihood and impact approach, in line with the risk assessment process described in ISO 31000.

## 1. Identify

Each risk is written as a short event, with its **cause** and its **consequence** recorded separately. This keeps the mitigation focused on the cause rather than the symptom.

> Risk: Unauthorized vehicle entry at main gate
> Cause: Manual paper gate log; no plate recognition
> Consequence: Theft or sabotage inside the yard

## 2. Score

### Likelihood

| Score | Level | Definition |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Not expected in the next 3 years |
| 2 | Unlikely | Could happen once in 3 years |
| 3 | Possible | Could happen once a year |
| 4 | Likely | Expected several times a year |
| 5 | Almost certain | Expected monthly or more |

### Impact

| Score | Level | People | Financial | Operations |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minimal | No injury | Under AED 5k | No delay |
| 2 | Minor | First aid | Under AED 50k | Delay under 4 h |
| 3 | Moderate | Lost-time injury | Under AED 250k | Delay under 24 h |
| 4 | Major | Serious injury | Under AED 1m | Client penalty |
| 5 | Severe | Fatality | Over AED 1m | Site closure |

When a risk affects more than one column, the highest applicable impact is used. Financial thresholds are illustrative for this fictional site and should be set per client.

## 3. Rate

**Score = Likelihood × Impact**

| Rating | Score | Required response |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 1–4 | Accept and monitor |
| Medium | 5–9 | Mitigate within normal planning |
| High | 10–14 | Mitigation plan with an owner and due date |
| Critical | 15–25 | Escalate to management; act immediately |

## 4. Treat

- **Inherent risk** is scored with the existing controls only.
- Every High or Critical risk gets a **mitigation action**, an **owner** (by role) and a **due date**.
- **Residual risk** is scored assuming the mitigation is fully in place.
- **Reduction** = 1 − residual score ÷ inherent score.

## 5. Monitor

- Status is one of Open, In progress or Done.
- An action is **overdue** when the due date is before the as-of date and the status is not Done.
- The register is reviewed monthly and after any incident, and every score is re-checked at least once a quarter.

## Limits

- Scores are judgements made by the assessor. They are consistent within this register, not absolute.
- A 5×5 matrix compresses very different risks into the same cell. A Critical risk always gets a written plan, whatever its score.
